# EW VERDORA — Occasion Calendar sales playbook (Kuching)

Operational guide for Agent drafts and Owner planning, not a statement that any school or organiser has confirmed its event date. Timezone: Asia/Kuching. All outbound text is an editable suggestion, never an automatic send.

## Shared rules

1. Use confirmed event dates for firm production planning. A pattern date must always say 预计 / estimated / anggaran. A not_found event must never be assigned a date.
2. Promotion start is displayDate minus order_lead_days; final event day is included. Never lock fresh flower inventory against a pattern or not_found date.
3. No Agent-created discounts, prices, payment accounts, guaranteed flowers, exact-copy promises, fake reviews, fake scarcity or competitor claims. Refer quotes to the official EW VERDORA process.
4. Do not reveal external production partners, costs or margins.
5. PDPA (Malaysia): use voluntarily supplied contact information for a stated purpose with appropriate notice and consent; reach out only to people who have opted in or where there is a lawful basis; honor opt-outs immediately. No school pupil lists, scraped private numbers, bulk unsolicited WhatsApp/DM, nor sharing contact databases.
6. For institutions, confirm event dates through official office contacts or public event notices. Record actual ceremony date separately from article publication date.
7. If customers want an order, ask for occasion, quantity, delivery area/time and budget. Final availability, pricing and fulfilment require official confirmation.

## Graduation — graduation

**Who pays:** graduating students, family, friends, alumni, parent groups; separately, schools may commission stage/ceremony arrangements.

**Where to find permitted demand:** school public announcements; university convocation pages; existing consenting parent/community customers; opt-in WhatsApp broadcasts; approved Agent social content. Do not harvest student names or contact details.

**Timeline:** 45–30 days before confirmed ceremony: research date and plan product mix; 21–14 days: promote preorder options; final 7–3 days: confirm quantities, pickup/delivery and payment through official channels.

**Sample scripts (editable):**
- 中文：毕业要送花的话，可以先告诉我典礼日期、预算和想送的人，我帮你看看合适的花束，再走 EW VERDORA 官方流程确认 😊
- English: Planning a graduation bouquet in Kuching? Share the ceremony date, recipient and budget, and I can help check suitable options through EW VERDORA.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Nak hadiahkan bunga untuk graduasi di Kuching? Boleh kongsi tarikh majlis, penerima dan bajet? Saya bantu semak pilihan melalui EW VERDORA.

**Non-discount hook:** matching card message, clear collection windows and accessible bouquet sizing; only claim services actually approved.

**Repeat customers:** ask consenting customers whether they want reminders for the next graduation in their family; keep a removable preference, not a school directory.

**Metrics:** verified ceremony coverage, opt-in enquiries, quotation-to-order conversion, fulfilment accuracy, timely quality complaints.

## Festivals — festival

**Who pays:** couples, families, employers and businesses depending on the holiday. Different seasons need different products; do not equate all holidays with roses.

**Where:** existing opted-in flower buyers; local community business pages; publicly advertised corporate gifting requirements; customer-initiated messages.

**Timeline:** 30–21 days: product planning and approved imagery; 14–7 days: active preorder reminders; 3–1 days: final confirmation and logistics. Holidays subject to official announcement remain estimated until confirmed.

**Scripts:**
- 中文：接下来如果有节日送花计划，可以先告诉我送给谁、日期和预算，我帮你确认款式与安排。
- English: If you're planning flowers for an upcoming celebration, tell me the recipient, date and budget. I can help confirm an appropriate arrangement.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Kalau ada perancangan hadiah bunga untuk perayaan nanti, kongsi penerima, tarikh dan bajet. Saya boleh bantu semak pilihan sesuai.

**Non-discount hook:** early reservation request, gift-card wording and clear delivery coverage, subject to current Owner policy.

**Repeat customers:** consent-based yearly occasion reminder or preference check; no unsolicited bulk sales messages.

**Metrics:** seasonal enquiry rate, confirmed preorders, on-time delivery, repeat purchase and unsold stems.

## Cultural and religious occasions — cultural

**Who pays:** individuals, families, congregations and formal event organisers. Remembrance and religious observance require respectful language.

**Where:** open institutional event requests, prior customers who opted in, church/temple offices through professional introductions, public noticeboards.

**Timeline:** 21–14 days: verify dates and appropriateness; 10–7 days: present suitable options; 3–1 days: final delivery and care instructions.

**Scripts:**
- 中文：如果这个纪念或礼仪场合需要用花，可以告诉我用途、地点和日期，我帮你确认合适的安排。
- English: If flowers are needed for a ceremony or remembrance, please share the purpose, venue and date so I can help check an appropriate arrangement.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Jika perlukan bunga untuk majlis atau acara memperingati, kongsi tujuan, lokasi dan tarikh supaya saya boleh bantu semak gubahan yang sesuai.

**Non-discount hook:** respectful card wording, appropriate floral options and confirmed venue logistics.

**Repeat customers:** only event-organiser follow-ups with consent; avoid intrusive remembrance marketing.

**Metrics:** appropriate product selection, confirmation accuracy, service complaints and referrals.

## Business openings — business

**Who pays:** shop founders, partner businesses, suppliers, peers and well-wishers.

**Where:** publicly announced shop openings, chambers of commerce, customers who initiate enquiries, relevant business community groups according to group rules. Verify public opening dates before referencing them.

**Timeline:** 30–14 days: identify publicly announced openings; 14–7 days: offer approved stands; final 3 days: confirm card wording, placement, collection and payment.

**Scripts:**
- 中文：看到你们准备开业，恭喜！如果需要开张花篮，可以告诉我开幕日期、地点和祝福署名，我帮你确认安排。
- English: Congratulations on the upcoming opening! If you need a congratulatory flower stand, share the opening date, location and card name, and I can check the arrangements.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Tahniah atas pembukaan yang akan datang! Jika perlukan jambangan atau stand bunga, kongsi tarikh, lokasi dan nama pada kad, saya bantu semak.

**Non-discount hook:** card layout preview, signage check and delivery coordination where approved.

**Repeat customers:** business anniversary reminders only for customers who agreed to receive them.

**Metrics:** opening leads with verifiable dates, qualified enquiries, average fulfilment time, corporate repeat orders.

## Weddings — wedding

**Who pays:** wedding couple and sometimes families or professional planners.

**Where:** public wedding fair vendor enquiries, bridal planners, venues, customer referrals and inbound social enquiries. Never use private wedding guest lists.

**Timeline:** 90–60 days: discovery and mood board; 60–30 days: official scope/quote; 30–14 days: final flower quantities, setup and dismantling; 7 days: final logistics.

**Scripts:**
- 中文：婚礼花艺可以先从场地、日期、预算和主要布置区域开始，我帮你整理需求，再安排正式报价。
- English: For wedding flowers, start with the date, venue, budget and key areas to decorate. I can organise the brief for an official quote.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Untuk bunga perkahwinan, boleh mula dengan tarikh, lokasi, bajet dan kawasan hiasan utama. Saya bantu susun keperluan untuk sebut harga rasmi.

**Non-discount hook:** clear itemised scope, mock layout discussion and reuse planning where feasible and approved.

**Repeat customers:** post-event referrals and anniversary preferences only with opt-in.

**Metrics:** enquiry-to-quote conversion, quote completeness, setup punctuality, budget variance and satisfaction.

## Corporate, fairs and other events — event

**Who pays:** event organisers, corporate procurement, professional associations, government/event operations teams.

**Where:** publicly announced BCCK and venue schedules, organiser websites, published tender/RFQ notices, business-network referrals and corporate inbound enquiries.

**Timeline:** 60–30 days: confirm event date/venue and procurement channel; 30–14 days: submit official scope/quote; final 7–2 days: stage layout, delivery timing, onsite contacts and final approvals.

**Scripts:**
- 中文：活动如果需要签到台、主桌或舞台用花，可以发我活动日期、场地和数量，我帮你整理一份正式需求清单。
- English: If you need stage, reception or VIP table flowers, share your event date, venue and approximate quantities. I can help prepare a formal requirements list.
- Bahasa Malaysia: Jika perlukan bunga untuk pentas, kaunter pendaftaran atau meja VIP, kongsi tarikh, tempat dan anggaran kuantiti. Saya boleh bantu sediakan senarai keperluan rasmi.

**Non-discount hook:** single point of contact, delivery/run-of-show coordination and clear quotation scope, where available.

**Repeat customers:** permission-based quarterly check-in on upcoming approved company events; honor communication preferences.

**Metrics:** verified event leads, RFQ response time, project contribution after delivery costs, repeat business and fulfilment issues.

## Calendar operating rhythm

- Owner reviews the pending proof queue on working days. A posted article date is NOT necessarily a graduation date.
- Agents may submit publicly viewable proof; cannot approve their own evidence.
- For high-demand events, getUrgent returns at most three occasions currently inside their configured promotion window.
- Pattern records are for outreach and date verification, not a firm stock commitment.
- Daily tasks are an optional AI-assisted draft with a deterministic fallback. A person must review and choose to send.
- Owner should compare forecast with actual order volume before changing purchasing quantities.
